# Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard 📊

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales performance, track key revenue metrics, and evaluate product categories.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Tool:** Power BI Desktop (`.pbix`)
* **Features:** Data Modeling, Visualizations, Dynamic Slicers & Filters

## 🔑 Key Insights & Capabilities
* **KPI Tracking:** Visualized total sales, total revenue, and overall profitability.
* **Trend Analysis:** Tracked periodic sales trends and revenue growth patterns.
* **Product Performance:** Identified top-performing products and sales channels to drive business insights.
